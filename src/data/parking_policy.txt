                            GENERAL INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Stargate Parking Facility is a modern, state-of-the-art multi-level parking 
structure designed to serve the downtown business district, retail center, and 
surrounding entertainment venues. Our facility offers secure, convenient, and 
affordable parking solutions for daily commuters, shoppers, event attendees, 
and long-term parkers alike.

Facility Overview:
- Total Capacity: 1,850 parking spaces across 6 levels
- Structure Type: Multi-level covered parking garage
- Year Opened: 2019
- Security: 24/7 monitored with CCTV surveillance
- Accessibility: Full ADA compliance with designated accessible spaces

Operating Hours:
- Daily Operations: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
- Staffed Booth Hours: 6:00 AM - 12:00 AM (Midnight)
- After-Hours Exit: Automated payment kiosks and mobile app available

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                           BOOKING PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

OPTION 1: DRIVE-UP (No Reservation Required)

1. Approach any open entry lane
2. Take a ticket from the automated dispenser OR scan your monthly permit
3. Gate will open automatically
4. Park in any available space appropriate for your vehicle type
5. Upon exit, pay at a kiosk or staffed booth, then proceed to exit gate

OPTION 2: ONLINE RESERVATION (Recommended for Events)

Step 1 - Visit Website or App
Go to www.stargateparking.com or download the Stargate Parking app from the 
App Store or Google Play.

Step 2 - Select Your Details
- Choose your arrival date and time
- Select estimated duration
- Pick your preferred parking level/type
- Review pricing

Step 3 - Complete Payment
- Enter payment information
- Apply any discount codes or validations
- Receive confirmation email with QR code

Step 4 - Arrival
- Scan QR code at entry gate OR enter confirmation number
- Gate opens automatically
- Park in designated reserved section or any available space

Step 5 - Departure
- Simply scan QR code at exit gate
- Gate opens automatically (prepaid)

OPTION 3: MONTHLY PERMIT APPLICATION

Step 1 - Submit Application
Complete the monthly permit application online at www.stargateparking.com/permits
or visit our management office during business hours.

Required Information:
- Full name and contact details
- Vehicle information (make, model, year, color, license plate)
- Preferred permit type
- Payment method for recurring billing

Step 2 - Processing
Applications are typically processed within 2-3 business days. You will 
receive email confirmation upon approval.

Step 3 - Permit Issuance
- Digital permits are linked to your license plate (no physical tag needed)
- RFID transponder available for $25 (optional, for faster entry)
- Permits activate on the 1st of the selected month

Step 4 - Usage
- Entry/exit via license plate recognition or RFID scan
- Access your assigned level or space type
- Monthly billing on the 1st of each month

OPTION 4: CORPORATE ACCOUNTS

Businesses with 10 or more vehicles can apply for corporate accounts featuring:
- Discounted monthly rates
- Consolidated billing
- Online account management portal
- Dedicated parking section
- Flexible add/remove vehicle capability

Contact: corporate@stargateparking.com or call (555) 782-7428 ext. 2


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                        POLICIES & REGULATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GENERAL PARKING RULES

1. Obey all posted signs, signals, and speed limits (5 MPH throughout facility)
2. Park within designated space lines only
3. Do not park in spaces designated for other uses (ADA, EV, reserved, etc.)
4. Turn off engine when parked; no idling for extended periods
5. Lock your vehicle and take all valuables with you
6. Headlights must be on at all times while driving in the facility
7. No honking except for emergencies
8. Pedestrians have the right of way at all times

VEHICLE REQUIREMENTS

Permitted Vehicles:
- Standard passenger vehicles (cars, SUVs, trucks under clearance limit)
- Motorcycles (designated area only)
- Oversized vehicles in designated sections only
- Commercial vehicles with prior approval

Prohibited Vehicles:
- Recreational vehicles (RVs) and trailers
- Vehicles leaking fluids
- Vehicles without valid registration
- Vehicles exceeding posted clearance heights

ENTRY & EXIT POLICIES

- Each entry ticket/reservation is valid for ONE vehicle only
- Do not share tickets or access credentials
- Lost tickets will be charged the daily maximum rate
- Tailgating through gates is prohibited and monitored
- Re-entry requires a new ticket unless you have a monthly permit

PAYMENT POLICIES

Payment Due: At time of exit for hourly/daily parking
Grace Period: 15 minutes after payment before exit
Lost Ticket Fee: Daily maximum rate applies
Declined Payment: Vehicle must remain until valid payment is made
Disputed Charges: Contact customer support within 30 days for review
Refunds: Prepaid reservations may be cancelled up to 2 hours before 
         arrival for full refund; no refunds for no-shows

MONTHLY PERMIT POLICIES

Billing Cycle: 1st of each month, auto-charged to payment method on file
Late Payment: $25 late fee after 5-day grace period
Failed Payment: Access suspended after 10 days; reinstated upon payment
Cancellation: Must notify by the 25th of the month for next month cancellation
Permit Transfer: Permits are non-transferable between individuals
Vehicle Changes: Update vehicle information online or notify management; 
                 up to 2 vehicle changes per month at no charge
Lost RFID Transponder: $25 replacement fee

OVERNIGHT & EXTENDED PARKING

- Vehicles may remain overnight; daily rates continue to accrue
- Maximum continuous parking: 14 consecutive days
- Vehicles parked beyond 14 days without notification may be considered 
  abandoned and towed at owner's expense
- Extended parking arrangements available; contact management

ABANDONED VEHICLES

A vehicle is considered abandoned if:
- Parked for more than 14 consecutive days without arrangement
- Appears inoperable or has expired registration beyond 30 days
- Owner cannot be contacted after reasonable attempts

Abandoned vehicles will be towed and stored at owner's expense. Retrieval 
instructions will be posted at the management office.

ADA ACCESSIBILITY POLICY

- 32 ADA-accessible spaces available on Level 1 near elevators
- Valid disabled parking placard or plate required
- Misuse of accessible spaces is prohibited and may result in towing
- Accessible routes to elevators and exits are clearly marked
- Service animals are welcome throughout the facility

EV CHARGING POLICIES

- EV charging spaces are for actively charging vehicles only
- Maximum charging session: 4 hours (to ensure availability)
- Idle fee of $0.10/minute applies after charging is complete
- Non-EV vehicles parked in EV spaces will be subject to $50 fine
- Report malfunctioning chargers to customer service immediately

MOTORCYCLE PARKING POLICIES

- Motorcycles must park in designated motorcycle area on Level 1
- One motorcycle per space; no doubling up
- Secure your motorcycle with your own lock
- Standard entry ticket required; motorcycle rates apply

OVERSIZED VEHICLE POLICIES

- Vehicles over 7 ft tall or 20 ft long must use oversized sections
- Oversized sections located on Levels 4 and 6
- Higher rates apply to oversized vehicles
- Clearance must be verified before entering standard levels

PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES

The following activities are strictly prohibited:
- Smoking, vaping, or open flames anywhere in the facility
- Consumption of alcohol or controlled substances
- Vehicle repairs, maintenance, or car washing
- Loitering, soliciting, or panhandling
- Reckless driving, racing, or stunt driving
- Camping or sleeping in vehicles overnight
- Dumping trash or hazardous materials
- Photography or filming for commercial purposes without permit
- Playing loud music or causing disturbances
- Weapons of any kind (except law enforcement)
- Leaving pets unattended in vehicles

SECURITY & SAFETY

24/7 Security Features:
- CCTV surveillance throughout all levels
- Security patrol vehicles and personnel
- Emergency call stations on every level (blue light phones)
- Well-lit parking areas and walkways
- License plate recognition at all entry/exit points

Customer Safety Tips:
- Always lock your vehicle and close windows completely
- Do not leave valuables visible in your vehicle
- Note your parking level and space number
- Use well-lit walkways and elevators
- Report suspicious activity to security immediately
- Program our emergency number into your phone: (555) 782-7429

Emergency Procedures:
- In case of fire, evacuate via stairwells (do not use elevators)
- Fire extinguishers located on each level near elevators
- AED devices located at Level 1 customer service and Level 3 restroom area
- For medical emergencies, call 911 then notify security

LIABILITY & INSURANCE

Stargate Parking Facility provides space rental only. We are NOT responsible 
for:
- Theft of vehicles or contents
- Damage to vehicles from any cause including other vehicles, falling 
  objects, weather, or vandalism
- Personal injury except where caused by facility negligence
- Loss of personal property

Customers are advised to:
- Maintain comprehensive auto insurance
- Consider additional coverage for valuable items
- Review their policy for garage parking coverage

DAMAGES & INCIDENTS

If your vehicle is damaged while parked:
1. Document the damage with photos immediately
2. Report to customer service or security within 24 hours
3. File a police report if vandalism or theft is suspected
4. Submit an incident report at management office
5. Contact your insurance company

If you damage facility property or another vehicle:
1. Do not leave the scene
2. Report immediately to security or customer service
3. Provide your contact and insurance information
4. Complete an incident report

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
                       FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: What if I lose my parking ticket?
A: Visit the customer service booth or any payment kiosk. You will be charged 
   the daily maximum rate. Keep your receipt as proof of payment.

Q: Can I pay with cash?
A: Cash payments are accepted at the staffed booth (6 AM - Midnight). 
   After hours, use credit/debit card, mobile payment, or the Stargate app.

Q: Is there a grace period after I pay?
A: Yes, you have 15 minutes to exit after payment. If you exceed this time, 
   additional charges may apply.

Q: What if my car won't start?
A: Contact security at the nearest emergency call station or call 
   (555) 782-7429. We can provide a jump start or help arrange roadside 
   assistance.

Q: Are there restrooms in the facility?
A: Yes, restrooms are located on Levels 3 and 4, near the elevator lobbies.

Q: Can I reserve a specific parking space?
A: Specific space reservations are available for monthly Reserved Space permit 
   holders. Daily parkers may reserve a general spot type but not a specific 
   space number.

Q: Is the facility open on holidays?
A: Yes, the facility is open 24/7/365. The management office is closed on 
   major holidays, but all automated services remain operational.

Q: What happens if my monthly payment fails?
A: You will be notified by email. You have a 10-day grace period to update 
   payment information before access is suspended.

Q: Can I have multiple vehicles on one monthly permit?
A: Standard permits cover one vehicle at a time. You may update your vehicle 
   information up to twice per month. For multiple simultaneous vehicles, 
   additional permits are required.

Q: Do you offer validated parking?
A: Yes, partner merchants can validate up to 2 hours free. Ask participating 
   retailers for validation stickers or codes.

Q: Is there parking for electric vehicles?
A: Yes, we have 36 EV charging spaces on Levels 2 and 3 with Level 2 and DC 
   Fast Charging options.

Q: What is your cancellation policy for reservations?
A: Cancel at least 2 hours before your scheduled arrival for a full refund. 
   No refunds for late cancellations or no-shows.

Q: How do I report a problem or file a complaint?
A: Contact customer support at (555) 782-7428 or support@stargateparking.com. 
   For urgent matters, speak with on-site security.
